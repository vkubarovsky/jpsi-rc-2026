## 1. The final formula is missing from the NLO paper. Here it is.

The NLO paper gives `delta_self` (8), `delta_vert` (12), `delta_soft` (13),
`delta_hard` only as an unevaluated integral (14), and the exact
Bardin–Shumeiko result (28). **There is no compact closed form for the total
ultrarelativistic correction** — the thing an experiment actually applies.

The right one is Eq. (24)/(26) of Ehlotzky & Mitter with the missing term
restored:

    delta(m_V, omega_cut) = -(4 alpha/pi) [ (1/2 - ln(m_V/m_l))(ln r + 1/3)
                                          + (1/2)(L_2(r) - pi^2/6)
                                          + 17/72
                                          + R(r)
                                          + r^2/16 - (3/8) r ]

    r      = 2 omega_cut / m_V
    R(r)   = (r - r^2/4 - 3/4) [ ln( (m_V/m_l) sqrt(1-r) ) - 1/2 ]
    L_2(x) = - int_0^x ln(1-t)/t dt

For muons add the electron-loop vacuum polarization = `delta_self` of their
Eq. (8) with `m_e`.

`r^2/16 - (3/8)r` is the missing piece. Equivalently `+(alpha/pi)(3r/2 - r^2/4)`
added to delta. It vanishes as r -> 0 and reaches `5 alpha/(4 pi) = 0.0029` at
r = 1.

Verification: reproduces their Table I to six digits at every r_cut in both
channels; independent of omega by construction; at r = 1 equals
`delta_self + 3 alpha/(4 pi)`, i.e. their FSIC Eq. (16); gives 0.026135 for
e+e- at r = 1, matching their Bardin–Shumeiko Table III exactly.

## 3. Table I of the NLO paper: m_V = 1.5 GeV is correct

and 2E = m_V because E = m_V/2 at resonance (stated below their Eq. 22). The
rows are marked rho at 765.0, omega at 782.0, phi at 1019.5 — meson masses. So
the last row, 1500.0, is m_V = 1.5 GeV, exactly what the NLO paper used.

Decisive check — delta_R depends on the mass only through `ln(2E/m)`:

What *is* wrong is their claim of agreement with that table: at r_max = 1 they
have 0.023891 against E&M's 0.021. They agree only in the small-r_max rows.

## 5. New finding: your k_max = M_V/2 contradicts your own Eq. (22)

The text above your Eq. (16) says the maximum photon energy is `k_max = M_V/2`.
That is the massless limit. The exact endpoint is

    omega_max = (M_V^2 - 4 m_l^2)/(2 M_V) = M_V/2 - 2 m_l^2/M_V

which is NLO Eq. (15) — **and it is already in your paper**, as Eq. (22),
`E_gamma = (M_Jpsi^2 - M_ee^2)/(2 M_Jpsi)` evaluated at `M_ee -> 2 m_l`. So
Eqs. (16) and (22) of your own paper disagree with each other.

No consequence for electrons. For muons `r_max` can only reach 0.99535, so the
r_max = 1 row of a muon table is outside phase space. Not fixed — your call.
